# PR-085 — EUR-Lex Temporal Constraints and Deadlines

Adds deterministic extraction of explicit temporal constraints from
compliance rules and rule qualifiers.

Supported forms include:

- `within 30 days`;
- `within five years of publication`;
- `no later than 15 March 2027`;
- `no earlier than 2027-01-01`;
- `before`, `after`, `from` and `until`;
- `for a period of five years`;
- `every six months`;
- `annually`, `monthly` and `quarterly`.

Temporal constraints preserve their originating rule, requirement, source
node, source text and optional qualifier.

Extraction remains explicit and non-computational. The implementation does
not calculate deadlines, business days, holidays, time zones, extensions,
suspensions or implied temporal rules.
