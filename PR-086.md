# PR-086 — EUR-Lex Quantitative Thresholds

Adds deterministic extraction of explicit quantitative thresholds from compliance
rules and rule qualifiers.

Supported examples:

- at least EUR 5 000 000
- more than 100 employees
- less than 10 %
- between 5 % and 10 %
- from 20 employees to 50 employees

No conversions, inference or calculations are performed.
