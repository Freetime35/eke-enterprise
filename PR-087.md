# PR-087 — EUR-Lex Legal Consequences

## Summary
Adds deterministic extraction of explicit legal consequences from EUR-Lex rules.

## Scope
- Explicit sanctions and legal consequences only.
- Source-backed extraction.
- Links to quantitative thresholds (PR-086).
- Links to temporal constraints (PR-085).

## Out of scope
- Penalty calculation.
- Legal reasoning.
- Implicit consequences.
