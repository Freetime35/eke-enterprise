# PR-068 — EUR-Lex Legal Lifecycle and Amendment Dates

Adds:

- source-backed legal lifecycle events;
- adoption, signature, notification, publication and application milestones;
- entry-into-force, taking-effect and end-of-validity milestones;
- transposition, repeal and withdrawal dates;
- explicitly dated amendment events with both CELEX identifiers;
- deterministic deduplication and chronological ordering;
- metadata and RDF/XML parser integration;
- application and infrastructure tests;
- ADR-0063.

No migration or public HTTP endpoint change is required.
